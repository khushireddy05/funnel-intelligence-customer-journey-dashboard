# KPI Definitions

| KPI | Definition |
|---|---|
| Total prospects | Distinct customers at Initial Interaction |
| Total leads | Distinct customers at Lead |
| Qualified leads | Distinct customers at Qualified Lead |
| Total purchases | Distinct customers with an Initial purchase |
| Overall conversion rate | Initial purchasers ÷ prospects |
| Stage conversion rate | Distinct customers at current stage ÷ distinct customers at previous stage |
| Drop-off rate | 1 − stage conversion rate |
| Average stage duration | Average days from preceding stage in FactFunnelEvents |
| Resolution rate | Resolved cases ÷ all customer-care cases |
| Average resolution time | Mean resolution hours for resolved cases |
| Customer satisfaction | Mean satisfaction score across care cases |
| Repeat-purchase rate | Customers with Repeat purchase ÷ customers with Initial purchase |

All customer journey KPIs use distinct customers, not raw interaction counts, unless explicitly stated otherwise.
