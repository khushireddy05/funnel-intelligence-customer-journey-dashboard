# Dashboard Specification

## Global controls

Use a consistent filter pane with Date, Region, Country, Product, Customer Segment, Customer Type, and Marketing Channel. Apply filters across pages when meaningful.

## Pages

1. **Executive Overview** — KPI cards for prospects, leads, purchases, conversion, revenue, satisfaction, and repeat-purchase rate; monthly trend; compact funnel.
2. **Funnel Analysis** — Full stage funnel, stage conversion/drop-off table, average stage duration, and breakdowns by product, region, channel, and segment.
3. **Marketing Analysis** — Prospects, leads, qualified leads, and purchasers by marketing channel and campaign; channel-quality comparison.
4. **Sales Analysis** — Opportunities, offers, purchases, revenue, sales-cycle duration, product mix, and regional performance.
5. **Customer Care** — Case volume, resolution rate, resolution time, satisfaction, contact channels, and case categories.
6. **Loyalty Analysis** — Initial vs repeat purchasers, repeat-purchase rate, time to repeat purchase, and repeat behavior by product/segment.

## Design principles

- Keep KPI definitions visible through tooltips or an info panel.
- Use a funnel chart only for ordered acquisition stages.
- Use bar charts for categorical comparisons and line charts for time trends.
- Allow drill-through from an aggregate chart to customer-level records only if appropriate for the privacy context.
